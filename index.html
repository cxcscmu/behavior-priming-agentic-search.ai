Beneficial Reasoning Behaviors in Agentic Search and Effective Post-Training to Obtain Them

Identifying and Instilling Critical Reasoning Behaviors for Search Agents

Jiahe Jin, Abhijay Paladugu, Chenyan Xiong

Language Technologies Institute, Carnegie Mellon University

📄 Paper (arXiv) 📥 PDF 💻 Code

Abstract

Agentic search leverages large language models (LLMs) to interpret complex user information needs and execute a multi-step process of planning queries, executing searches, and adapting its strategy based on retrieved documents. This introduces unique challenges for LLMs' reasoning and agentic capabilities in interacting with retrieval systems and the broader web. In this paper, we design a reasoning-driven LLM-based pipeline to study effective behaviors in agentic search. Using this pipeline, we analyze successful agentic search trajectories and extract four beneficial reasoning behaviors: Information Verification, Authority Evaluation, Adaptive Search, and Error Recovery. We then propose a new behavior priming technique to train more effective agentic search models. It synthesizes agentic search trajectories with target behaviors and integrates them into the agentic search model through supervised finetuning (SFT), followed by standard reinforcement learning (RL). Experiments on three benchmarks—GAIA, WebWalker, and HLE—demonstrate that behavior priming significantly improves agentic search accuracy, yielding over 35% gains in Llama3.2-3B and Qwen3-1.7B after RL. Ablation studies further show that priming effective behaviors is more beneficial than emphasizing result correctness: finetuning on trajectories with desirable reasoning behaviors but incorrect results outperforms training on trajectories with correct answers.

🎯 Four Beneficial Reasoning Behaviors

🔍 Information Verification

Validating results across multiple sources and cross-checking information to ensure accuracy.

Example:
"My task is clear: verify if the quoted text exactly matches Greetham's article. Accuracy is paramount; I'll use 'uncoupled', 'authors', 'mis-transmission', and 'veil' to zero in on the relevant section."

⚖️ Authority Evaluation

Assessing source reliability and resolving conflicts by weighting reputable sources.

Example:
"I'm aiming for the USGS's own reports or databases, like the 'Nonindigenous Aquatic Species' page, to get the most reliable data."

🔄 Adaptive Search

Modifying search strategies dynamically based on intermediate results and feedback.

Example:
"The search engine may not have indexed the quote perfectly, or the user's quote may differ slightly. I'll refine my strategy."

🛠️ Error Recovery

Detecting and correcting mistakes through strategic pivots and backtracking.

Example:
"My initial search didn't deliver the goods... I need to get more precise."

💡 Key Contributions

  • Automated Behavior Discovery: We design an LLM-based pipeline that automatically identifies beneficial reasoning behaviors by comparing successful and unsuccessful agentic search trajectories.
  • Behavior Priming Method: We propose a post-training technique that systematically instills identified behaviors through supervised fine-tuning (SFT) followed by reinforcement learning (RL).
  • Surprising Finding: Reasoning behaviors matter more than answer correctness — SFT on trajectories with desired behaviors but incorrect answers achieves comparable performance to SFT on correct-answer trajectories after RL.
  • Mechanistic Insights: Behavior priming enhances exploration capabilities (higher pass@k and entropy) and test-time scaling, establishing a robust foundation for effective RL training.

🔬 Method Overview

Behavior Priming Pipeline

  1. Trajectory Generation: Generate diverse agentic search trajectories using a powerful model (Gemini-2.5-Flash).
  2. Behavior Identification: Use LLM-based analysis to compare successful vs. failed trajectories and extract beneficial behaviors.
  3. Behavior Mining: Run a reasoner → extractor → merger pipeline to consolidate and quantify behavior patterns.
  4. SFT with Behavior Priming: Fine-tune models on trajectories exhibiting the four identified behaviors.
  5. RL Training: Apply standard reinforcement learning to further improve performance.
